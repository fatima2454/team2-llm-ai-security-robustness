# Cyber Atlas - Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 PTO days per year, rising to 20 days after three years of service. Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Unused PTO rolls over up to a five-day cap each January.
