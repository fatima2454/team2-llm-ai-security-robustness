# Cyber Atlas - Expense Reimbursement Policy

Submit expense reports in the finance portal within 30 days of purchase and attach itemized receipts. Approved business travel, client meals, and home-office equipment are reimbursable; personal expenses are not. Reimbursements are paid on the next payroll cycle.
